Imagination becomes intelligence™

Wilson is a free AI companion that lives in your browser. Talk or type, think out loud, plan, explore ideas — and he remembers what matters to you.

Open Wilson — it's free

No account needed. No app store. Works on iPhone, Android and desktop — add it to your Home Screen for one-tap access.

Free, sign-in optional

Open the link and start talking. No payment. Create an account only if you want your chats on any device.

Voice and text

Speak or type. Wilson can answer out loud, help organize reminders and notes, and pull live public info like weather.

Your data stays yours

Sign-in is optional. If you sign in, your chats sync to your account so you can pick them up anywhere. Never sold, no ads, and you can delete your account and chats anytime.

The Only One

“+ The Only One” is you. Every person's experience with Wilson is their own — shaped by your conversations, your goals and your way of thinking. Same Wilson, a different story for everyone.
